## Context

Orgni is **not** a document management platform.

It is **not** a document parser.

It is **not** a chatbot.

Orgni is an **Operational Intelligence Platform**.

Its purpose is to build and continuously maintain a living operational model of an organisation.

It transforms organisational evidence into structured organisational understanding.

Today, the first supported evidence sources are:

* invoices
* contracts
* proofs of payment

In the future it will also consume:

* Microsoft Teams
* Slack
* Email
* Meetings
* CRM systems
* ERP systems
* Internal applications
* AI agent activity

The architecture should already reflect this future without pretending those integrations already exist.

---

# Product Philosophy

Everything in the UI should answer one question:

> **What does Orgni currently understand about this organisation?**

Not:

> What documents have been uploaded?

Documents are simply one source of organisational evidence.

The organisational model is the product.
