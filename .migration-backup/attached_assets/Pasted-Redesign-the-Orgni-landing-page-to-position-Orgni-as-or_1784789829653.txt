Redesign the Orgni landing page to position Orgni as organisational intelligence infrastructure developed by Olyxee.

The page must target business leaders first, while still making it clear that Orgni can also provide context to applications and AI agents.

The current website should not feel like a document tool, chatbot, generic AI SaaS product or collection of disconnected modules.

## Main objective

The visitor should understand within the first few seconds that:

1. Orgni helps organisations prepare for and adopt AI.
2. Orgni creates a live, evidence-backed understanding of how the organisation operates.
3. It connects documents, systems, people, policies, workflows, obligations and events.
4. Business teams, applications and AI agents can use the same trusted context.
5. Orgni is infrastructure developed by Olyxee, not just another standalone SaaS tool.

## Visual direction

Use a clean enterprise infrastructure style.

Design requirements:

* White or very light neutral background
* Dark near-black text
* One primary blue accent
* Subtle secondary colours only where they improve hierarchy
* Large amounts of whitespace
* Clear section separation
* Large, readable typography
* Short paragraphs
* Rounded corners used sparingly
* Thin borders
* Very subtle shadows
* No decorative icons
* No emojis
* No AI-generated people
* No excessive gradients
* No glowing effects
* No crowded feature card grids
* No floating chatbot in the hero
* No animations that distract from the message

The page should feel similar to a modern cloud infrastructure or enterprise platform company, not a consumer AI application.

## Navigation

Keep the navigation simple:

* Platform
* Use Cases
* Infrastructure
* Research
* Company
* Request a Demo

The Olyxee name should appear clearly as the company developing Orgni.

Do not add separate navigation items for Orgni Docs, Orgni Finance, Orgni Workflows or Orgni Assistant.

These should not appear as separate products.

## Important copy rules

Use plain business language.

Avoid phrases such as:

* Revolutionary AI
* Supercharge productivity
* AI-powered everything
* Intelligent automation platform
* Next-generation SaaS
* One platform for everything

Do not describe Orgni as a chatbot.

Do not make the homepage mainly about document upload.

Do not present the tokenizer, ontology or document intelligence pipeline as separate products.

These are internal infrastructure components.

## Responsive behaviour

The mobile version must preserve the same message order.

On smaller screens:

* Stack diagrams vertically
* Keep headings short
* Avoid horizontal overflow
* Keep buttons full-width where appropriate
* Maintain generous spacing
* Do not reduce text until it becomes difficult to read

## Final result

The redesigned page should make Orgni feel like a serious enterprise infrastructure company.

The core message should be:

Orgni creates and maintains the shared organisational context that humans, applications and AI agents need to understand and operate within a business reliably.
